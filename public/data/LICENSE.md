# Geographic data attribution and redistribution

- `angles-terrain.json`: **IGN LiDAR HD MNT**, acquisition **2023-04-09**, edition **2025-01-24**, retrieved 2026-11-10. Native grid 0.5 m, Lambert-93 / IGN69; browser samples at **10 m**, not 10 m accuracy. [Official dataset](https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/mnt-lidar-hd/), [Licence Ouverte 2.0](https://www.etalab.gouv.fr/wp-content/uploads/2017/04/ETALAB-Licence-Ouverte-v2.0.pdf). Per-tile URLs, original/normalized hashes and CRS repair are retained in provenance and `docs/mission-08-5-sources.json`.
- `angles-environment.json`: clipped, topology-preserving derivatives of **IGN BD TOPO V3 vegetation**, **IGN BD Forêt V2**, **IGN OCS GE Vienne 2023**, and **ASP / IGN RPG 2024**, Licence Ouverte 2.0. Native identifiers/attributes, individual source URLs, dates and checksums accompany the file. Unknown observation dates remain null; the BD Forêt department delivery is dated 2014-04-01, not a newly observed 2026 forest. OCS GE 2023 archive checksum was verified against IGN's MD5. Positions/size/species of generated plants are procedural; a leaf-type class describes cover, not every tree.
- `angles-terrain-lod.bin`: derived triangulation of the same IGN MNT grid; no new measured heights.
- `angles-osm.geojson`: **© OpenStreetMap contributors**, extracted 2026-11-08, [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). Retain attribution and make the OSM-derived database available under ODbL on redistribution. The original two woodlands retain **Union européenne – SOeS, CORINE Land Cover, 2006**; the newer normalized coverage replaces them only when validated. Missing normalized data falls back to those documented OSM habitats.
- `angles-landcover.png`: a combined derived database of normalized IGN/ASP classes and retained OSM uses; redistributed with its source databases and the ODbL obligations for the OSM-derived combination, while original IGN/ASP sources retain their open licence and attribution. Colors are original procedural art. Raster spacing of 2 m and its artistic alpha are not survey accuracy or confidence.
- `angles-surface.pack/json`: common triangulated partition of OSM road/water outlines and IGN source ground. OSM-derived database under ODbL, IGN heights with Licence Ouverte 2.0 attribution. Estimated road widths and materials remain interpretations. Water follows the DEM for visualization; no measured river level, discharge or bathymetry is asserted. The bridge and ruins remain schematic; no arches or unverified monument dimensions have been added.
- `angles-road-edge.pack/json`: distances to the same estimated OSM road union; ODbL-derived database, not surveyed shoulders. The existing regional road texture/material atlas remains original procedural work.

IGN orthophoto, OCS cartographic reference images, BD TOPAGE 2026 and a Copernicus TCD 2018 crop were acquired for audit/reference, not embedded as game textures or substituted for the selected sources. Reference evidence retains its own URLs and licences in the Mission 08.5 source manifest. Copernicus: European Union / Copernicus Land Monitoring Service, EEA, Tree Cover Density 2018; exported/reprojected by the public EEA ImageServer, [data policy](https://land.copernicus.eu/en/data-policy). The tested TCD product covers Europe; it is not a global forest inventory.

Rebuild with `npm run geo:prepare`, `npm run geo:roads`, `node scripts/prepare-road-edges.mjs`. Heavy originals are intentionally excluded from Git. See `docs/mission-08-5.md` and `docs/GEOGRAPHIC_IMPORT.md` for exact integration, confidence, limitations and reproducible import instructions. Application code and procedural assets remain separate from geographic databases.
