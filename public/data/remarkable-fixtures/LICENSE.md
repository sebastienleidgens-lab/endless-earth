# Synthetic validation fixtures — CC0 1.0

The geometry, heights, local objects and derived light packs in this directory
are original synthetic validation data, dedicated under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

They describe no real place, survey, geographic position or actual monument.
Their vertical datum is explicitly `SYNTHETIC-METRES`, never IGN69.
Source: `src/fixtures/remarkable.mjs`; reproducible packs:
`node scripts/prepare-remarkable-fixtures.mjs`.

The viewer uses the existing shared Endless Earth engine and regional artistic
materials, which retain their repository/source terms. No photograph, paid 3D
asset, outside geographic data or copy of the Angles castle was acquired.
