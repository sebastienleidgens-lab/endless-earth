# Angles landmarks — provenance and rights

The local meshes and bridge underlay are prepared derivatives, not exact surveys.
See [Mission 14](../../docs/mission-14.md) and the provenance fields in
`angles-landmarks.json` for measured, reconstructed, interpreted and unknown parts.

- IGN LiDAR HD: acquisition 9 April 2023, edition 24 January 2025,
  Lambert-93 / IGN69, [Licence Ouverte 2.0](https://www.etalab.gouv.fr/licence-ouverte-open-licence/).
  A bounded classified COPC point extract supplies local height observations.
  The native 0.5 m MNT provides ground context.
- IGN BD TOPO: Géoplateforme WFS, Licence Ouverte 2.0. The selected bridge and road
  records document the bridge and carriageway width, with their stated precision.
- © OpenStreetMap contributors, [ODbL 1.0](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright):
  existing imported castle footprint, road/bridge axis and water outlines.
  The derived database is made available under ODbL 1.0; underlying IGN data
  retain their source attribution and Licence Ouverte 2.0 terms. This does not
  imply OSM or IGN approval of the reconstructed geometry.
- Mesh generation code and interpreted geometry follow the repository's terms;
  source database obligations above remain applicable.

No purchased or third-party 3D model is included. Documentary photographs are
not runtime textures or calibrated 3D assets. The two reduced Floppy36 photos in
`docs/mission-14-evidence/heritage/` remain CC BY-SA 4.0, with source links and
modifications in `reference-credits.json` and visible gallery captions. Previously
licensed Mission 13 references retain their original credits. ArmmA and private
collection images studied for context are not redistributed.
