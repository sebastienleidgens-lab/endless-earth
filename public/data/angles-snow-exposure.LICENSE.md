# Snow exposure derivative — provenance and rights

The upper envelope is prepared from the exact existing Mission 14/15A opaque
render geometry. It is an artistic shelter mask at 1.953125 m per pixel,
quantized to 1/64 m, not new elevation evidence, observed weather or snow depth.
It changes no geometry, building footprint, water, collision or walking support.
